# TEDx talk by Rebecca Okamoto 




I really like this approach. The key shift is: Don't just tell the interviewer what you are. Tell them what value you bring to their organization.
For your Full Stack AI Developer profile, let's combine Rebecca's 20-word sound bite with your 4+ years of experience.
1. Your 20-word power introduction
Recommended sound bite
Writing
I build scalable web applications and integrate Generative AI and Agentic AI to automate workflows, improve productivity, and solve real-world business problems.
This communicates:
    • What you do: Build scalable applications.
    • Your specialization: Generative AI and Agentic AI.
    • Your value: Automation, productivity, and business solutions.
2. Complete answer: "Tell me about yourself"
Now, use the 20-word introduction as your opening hook, followed by your experience.
Writing
Good morning, and thank you for this opportunity.
I'm [Your Name], and I help businesses build scalable web applications and leverage AI to automate workflows, improve productivity, and solve real-world problems.

In my current role, I work on designing, developing, and maintaining full-stack web applications, contributing across both frontend and backend development.
One of my key areas of contribution has been integrating Generative AI and Agentic AI capabilities into web applications, particularly for content generation and AI-powered workflows.
I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Applications from [College Name].
I'm passionate about exploring emerging technologies and translating them into practical solutions.
I'm now looking for an opportunity where I can bring my full-stack and AI expertise to build impactful products and contribute to business growth.
3. One important interview strategy
Think of your introduction in three layers:
    1. Hook: What value do you bring?
    2. Proof: What experience and technical skills support that value?
    3. Future: How can you contribute to the organization?
Don't try to explain everything in your introduction. Your goal is to make the interviewer curious enough to ask follow-up questions.
4. Let's personalize your power introduction
One important question:
What kind of problems do your AI-powered applications primarily solve?
For example, content generation, workflow automation, customer support, document processing, AI agents, or something else?
Your answer will help us create a much stronger and more distinctive 20-word introduction.
